What is STAT Mod?

STAT Mod turns Minecraft into a full RPG. Every action you take — fighting, mining, farming, crafting, taking hits — feeds a real character progression system: 23 stats, 84 unlockable perks, a 249-node magic tree, and an endless procedural dungeon dimension designed as endgame content. It runs fully standalone on NeoForge 1.21.1, and automatically deepens its gameplay when companion mods are installed.

⚔ Character progression you can feel

  • 23 stats (Strength, Agility, Vitality, Forging, Erudition, Arcane Power…) each level up through what you actually do: swing swords to raise Strength, sprint and dodge for Agility, survive damage for Vitality, smelt and craft for Forging. Your playstyle literally shapes your character.
  • 84 perks (6 tiers per active stat): reach stat thresholds to unlock passive bonuses — heavier melee hits, faster mining, longer sprint, better crafting yields. A miner and a duelist end up with visibly different characters.
  • Full GUI: a stat screen with XP bars for every stat, plus a talent-tree view (mirrored into Pufferfish's Skills if installed) where you can plan your build.

🏯 The Trial Dungeon — endless endgame

A dedicated dimension with infinite floors, each one a procedurally built fortress that must be conquered before the exit unlocks:

  • 100 rotating themes across 10 arcs — bone crypts, ice citadels, nether bastions, amethyst sanctums… each with matching architecture, mobs and traps. Floor 3 doesn't look or play like floor 47.
  • Three floor types: combat floors (wipe the wave), treasure floors every 5th (loot the vault), and boss floors every 10th — giant open-sky arenas where an altar summons bosses from a 40+ roster.
  • Mage enemies with real caster AI: pyromancers, cryomancers and clerics that kite you at range, telegraph their spells with sound and particles, retreat while facing you, and heal their squad. Hit a mage mid-cast to interrupt the spell — focusing the healer first actually matters.
  • Dungeon Rush scoring: chain kills within 8 seconds for a growing combo multiplier (up to ×2.5), random jackpots, and a ×2 "flawless floor" bonus if you clear without taking a hit. Your best combo and fastest clears are saved as personal records.
  • Risk and reward: every kill earns points scaled to the mob's difficulty and your depth. Dying costs you 25% of your points — but death in the dungeon never destroys your inventory or XP.
  • Secrets: hidden trapdoor rooms that are either blessed (bonus chests and buffs) or cursed (an ambush), and rare ultra-secret sanctuaries holding a unique relic weapon on a pedestal.
  • Co-op ready: floor conquest is shared with your FTB team, waves scale +50% mobs per extra player, and teammates earn assist points.

💰 A working economy

Dungeon points convert into a physical currency — actual coins and banknotes in your inventory. Spend them with specialized merchant NPCs inside the dungeon: the weaponsmith only sells weapons, the armorer only armor, the cleric only potions (each opens the shop locked to their own department). A magic banker villager stores your cash. The loop is simple and addictive: fight deeper → earn more → gear up → fight deeper.

🔮 Unified magic tree (with Iron's Spellbooks)

Install Iron's Spellbooks and STAT Mod becomes the progression authority for magic: a 249-node tree across 9 schools (fire, ice, lightning, holy, ender…), paid with magic points earned in play and gated by your Arcane Power and Erudition stats. Your mana pool is driven by your stats and persists properly across sessions.

🧩 Integrations (all optional — the mod works alone)

  • Iron's Spellbooks — unified magic tree, mana bridge, dungeon mage enemies
  • Tensura Reincarnated — races modify your base stats, soul level syncs with your global level
  • Pufferfish's Skills — beautiful mirrored UI for the perk tree
  • Epic Fight (experimental) — stat-gated combat skills
  • Overgeared — forge every weapon from every metal, gated by your Forging stat
  • Waystones / Lootr / L2 Hostility / PlayerRevive / FTB Teams — dungeon checkpoints, per-player loot chests, mob difficulty scaling, bleed-out revives in co-op, team-based conquest
