# Heated Materials Direction

Date: 2026-07-03
Scope: redefine the visual direction for `heated_*` materials before prompt production.

## Chosen Direction

- Fresh out of the furnace
- Strong internal heat read
- Core incandescent, edges slightly cooler
- Real forge material first, light magical identity second

## Visual Rules

- Not molten
- Not liquid
- Not a lava blob
- No flames around the object
- No giant aura
- No smoke cloud
- Strong inventory readability

## Shape Rules

- Shapes may differ by material
- They must still read clearly as forge materials
- Ingot-like materials stay dense and weighty
- Diamond stays gem-like but visibly heat-treated

## Magic Rules

- Mundane materials: heat only
- Magical materials: restrained fine magical lines or undertones only
- No floating runes
- No dominant glow over the heat read

## Material Intent

- `gold`, `tin`, `bronze`: practical heated forge stock
- `diamond`: heated crystalline forge gem
- `pyrium`: hotter and more volcanic, but controlled
- `arcane`: blue-violet magical metal under forge heat
- `mithril`: refined cool metal with noble heat treatment
- `magisteel*`: enchanted steel with subtle magical veins
- `orichalcum`, `hihiirokane`: premium heated legendary stock
- `adamantite`: dense dark mass with contained heat
